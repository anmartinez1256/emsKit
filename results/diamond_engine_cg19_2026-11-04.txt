4. Field layout of the GPU arrays (Mur box, GPU-only MC/s, three alternating runs each, quieter Mac)
   N-I-J-K (kept):  121^3 2589 2577 2573   201^3 2789 2782 2804
   I-J-K-N:         121^3 2408 2396 2350   201^3 2563 2564 2576
   N-I-J-K is 7-9 % faster: in I-J-K-N the three components of a cell are side by side, so a SIMD group's loads are
   48 B apart.

5. Excitation entries per tile
   Before: thread 0 of every tile scanned all excitation entries at every half-step. The box at 201^3 has 1035 voltage
   entries: GPU-only 1914 (tg 1024) / 2076 (tg 512) MC/s on the PEC box. The standalone prototype on the same
   exported operator: 2074 / 2305 (on model.py's operator, 450 entries: 2479 / 2639).
   After (per tile and phase, the entries inside the union of the tile's ranges, in entry order): 3135 / 3053.

6. Not on the GPU yet: UPML, lumped RLC, conducting sheets (Lorentz/Drude ADE), Debye, TFSF, steady state, cylinder.
   With any of them the engine prints the extension's name and runs the CPU loops.
