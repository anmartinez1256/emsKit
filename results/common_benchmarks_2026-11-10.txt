CORRECTION 2026-11-13 (benchmark cases without a source): in upstream master 1d710c2, benchmark/cases.py builds its uniform
meshes as arange(n) - (n-1)/2. With an even line count (bench.py's sizes S = 100 and M = 200 lines) no mesh line passes through the
origin, so the dipole that the cases `ideal`, `pml` and `dispersive` put at (0, 0, z) excites nothing (openEMS prints "Voltage
excitations: 0") and those three cases ran with all fields zero in section 1 below (part A and part B); `inhomogeneous`, `antenna` and `cylindrical`
have their own sources. The rates of those three cases below are therefore rates on zero fields. A control on 2026-11-13, on the M5 Pro, (results/
cpu_items_cg33_2026-11-13.txt, section 3 part c, with a source on the mesh: 101 / 201 lines) gave master's and the fork's rates within
the spread of the runs without a source on this chip (ideal M at 15 threads: master 3671 with a source and 3870 without, fork 3985 and
4071; dispersive M: master 2460 and 2437, fork 1338 and 1344), so the rates stand as rates; a "probes identical" on those three cases
says nothing, and the finding has not been reported upstream.

(patches 0001-0027; banner v0.37.0-44-g074a687), stock is openEMS v0.37.0 (snapshot `stock`).

The Metal diamond engine has no GPU version of the total-field/scattered-field extension and ran every step on
its CPU loops (its message says so): the same numbers as the multithreaded engine without flush-to-zero, at a quarter
of its speed (118 s against 27 s of time stepping, on a loaded machine).
With the tutorial's own end criterion (-50 dB of energy) the stop step is not reproducible (1104 and 1340 steps in
two stock runs, 782 with the fork at lambda/20: the engine tests the criterion between chunks of steps whose
length it sets from the speed it measures); the radar cross-sections of two such runs differ by up to 1.6e-3. Hence the fixed step counts.
