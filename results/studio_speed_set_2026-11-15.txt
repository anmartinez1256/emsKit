MACHINE: Mac Studio (Mac17,14), Apple M5 Max, 6 "Super" cores (hw.perflevel0, L2 of 16 MiB) + 12 "Performance" cores
(hw.perflevel1, L2 of 8 MiB), 32-core GPU, 36 GB, macOS 27.0 (26A425). The M5 tables of this repository were measured on an
Apple M5 MacBook Air (4 + 6 cores, 16 GB) and one set on an M5 Pro Mac mini (5 + 10 cores, 24 GB;
results/m5pro_speed_set_2026-11-12.txt); the numbers below are a third set and are compared only with each other.
When: 2026-11-15 06:49:04 to 08:23:01 (94 minutes), one slot of a quiet block on the machine (no other measured job beside
it), scripts/studio/studioset.zsh = the stages C, S, A, E, B, D in that order; every stage exited 0.
Libraries: stock = openEMS v0.37.0; cg31 = the fork's commit ff2538a (patches 0001-0039); cg33 = the fork's commit 13350ca
(patches 0001-0043; CPU engine "multithreaded", GPU engine "metal-diamond"), as snapshots for bench/bench.py; the same
library files as on the other two machines. "installed" in section 6 = cg33's build (installed on this machine 2026-11-14).
Method: bench/abtest.py, interleaved rounds, medians in MC/s; every run's port probes are compared with the reference run
(c0). Gate: wait up to 20 s for the other processes to use at most 300 % CPU (3 of 18 cores), redo a run once above that
(scripts/studio/common.zsh). Every row lists the other processes' median CPU / GPU use during its runs.
Background: 0.9 to 1.6 cores of other work during stage C (the speed table) and during the patch tutorial's rows of stage S
(1.2 to 1.6); 0.2 to 0.5 cores in the other stages. All processes together, the set included, 10 s samples: median 141 %
of one core over the block.
Verdicts: every CPU row is identical to the stock reference (probes, bit for bit); the Metal rows differ by at most 2.2e-7
relative (the CPU reference keeps subnormals, the GPU flushes them).

cg33: the Debye check, the six models identical to stock (18 of 18 rows each), validation 15 of 15, the Metal diamond check
72 of 72; Metal validation 15 of 15 and both boards end to end with the stop steps of the other machines (34300 and 50320);
