What it is
  OPENEMS_TBLOCK=k runs k timesteps on one tile of x-lines before moving on, and every thread works in that same tile (its share
  of the y lines), so the tile is read from memory once per k timesteps instead of once per timestep. Design after the cpu engine of
  upstream master (one cache-sized tile shared by all threads); none of its code. Per block: every tile [a,b) shrinks by one x-line
  per timestep on each inner side (a trapezoid), then the wedges between the tiles are filled. One spin barrier per task; the
  extensions' line hooks run in stages between two updates, on all threads, the x-lines of a stage cut into one share per thread so
  that all hooks of an x-line run on one thread in the one-thread order. No extension was changed.
  Switches: OPENEMS_TBLOCK=k|auto, OPENEMS_TBLOCK_W=n (tile width in x-lines), OPENEMS_TBLOCK_HOOKS=0 (hook stages on one
  thread; diagnostic), and the existing OPENEMS_BALANCE=0 (equal shares instead of measured ones).
  auto: the deepest k <= 8 whose tile of 2k+1 x-lines (24 B a cell, z padded to whole vectors) stays within 90 % of the cache the
  threads share (hw.perflevel0.l2cachesize, 16 MiB on the M5 and on the M5 Pro), a tile of at least 200000 cells; no tiling when
  the fields fit that cache or two timesteps of a tile do not. Choices (verbose runs): GCPW line and notch tutorial "the fields fit
  the cache"; SMA board k=8 W=32; 3 M box k=8 W=17; 1 M cube k=8 W=20; notch x3 k=8 W=27; patch x5 and the 8 M cube k=7 W=15;
  256^3 box k=4 W=9; a 64 M cube "two timesteps of a tile do not fit the cache".
  Refused, with a message: an extension that is not TS_LOCAL, coupled x-lines other than 0|1 and NX-2|NX-1, fewer x-lines than
  two tiles. Engine_Extension::GetMaxBlockDepth() caps k (steady-state detection: one signal period).

  - Against the fused sweep's best setting the tiled sweep is ahead on the two large grids with little extension work (patch x5
    x1.08, the 16.8 M box x1.23) and behind on the other four (3 M box x0.92, PML cube x0.85, notch x3 x0.81, SMA board x0.71).
  - It needs the 4 performance cores: at 8 or 10 threads it is behind the fused sweep at 8 threads on every model but the 16.8 M
    box. Measured shares against equal ones (OPENEMS_BALANCE=0) at 8 threads: within 3 % on every model.
  - The hook stages must run on all threads: on one thread (OPENEMS_TBLOCK_HOOKS=0) patch x5 2044 (all threads 2305), notch x3
    1499 (1861), PML cube 617 (1138).
  - The two boards of the project gain nothing: the GCPW line fits the cache (auto leaves the fused sweep), the SMA board loses.
  - Under heavy load from other programs (5.6 other cores busy, the morning's identity runs) the tiled sweep at 8 to 10 threads
    fell to 28 to 600 MC/s where the fused sweep at 4 threads held 730 to 1570: a barrier per task does not survive an
    oversubscribed machine. Hence opt-in, not a default.
