MACHINE: Mac Studio (Mac17,14), Apple M5 Max, 6 "Super" + 12 "Performance" cores, 36 GB. A set of its own ("studio");
compared only with numbers of this machine, never with the M5 MacBook Air or M5 Pro tables.

Why: after the y-banded fused sweep (cg34, results/large_grid_cg34_2026-11-22.txt) the dispersive case of upstream's
benchmark/bench.py was the one case of six still behind upstream master's `cpu` engine at 8 M and 30 M cells (0.75 of
1d710c2 and 0.64 to 0.66 of upstream's head ccb4472). About half of that case's cells are dispersive (a Lorentz block and a
Debye block). The packed hooks grouped four consecutive positions of an x-line, in a filled block four f4vectors with one
lane each: scalar gathers through twelve indices a group, and coefficients and state in one block that was read and written
back whole (about 132 bytes a Lorentz cell-step, 156 a Debye one, against 49 for a plain cell).

Code: one commit on the fork's branch speed-m5-gpu on top of 8d513ba (cg34): d3374c9, local at the date of this record (not
pushed, not installed). A group is the positions of one f4vector (base offset + lane mask) when a hook has at least 2.5
positions a group on average, else the position groups as before (conducting sheets); coefficients in a table of distinct
sets apart from the written state. OPENEMS_ADE_VEC=0 keeps position groups (2 forces vector groups), OPENEMS_ADE_COEF=
group|table|table32 picks the coefficient layout; both are test switches. Per-lane arithmetic unchanged.

Rule set before the run: keep only if the dispersive case at 30 M cells and 18 threads reaches 1.30 times cg34 and nothing
else (antenna case, the two boards, 1 M cells) loses more than 2 %. Result: 1.41 times; antenna 0.99 to 1.02; boards 1.005
at 18 threads (GCPW 4980 -> 5018, SMA 5432 -> 5445 MC/s, 4 paired rounds each). Kept.

layout. Resident memory is unchanged (the allocation is kept so a withdrawn grant needs none).
