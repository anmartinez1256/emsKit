The fork on a second machine: Apple M5 Pro (Mac mini), 2026-11-12
====================================================================================================
Bandwidth, thread sweep, speed table, allocation-placement valley, scheduling switches and the two boards end to end.
MACHINE: Mac mini, Apple M5 Pro (Mac17,16), 5 "Super" cores (hw.perflevel0, one L2 of 16 MiB) + 10 "Performance" cores
(hw.perflevel1, two L2 of 8 MiB), 24 GB, macOS 27.2.1 (26B131). Every other speed of this repository was measured on an Apple M5
MacBook Air (4 + 6 cores, 16 GB); these numbers are a set of their own and are compared only with each other.
When: 2026-11-12 07:13:43 to 08:32:36, one block of a night queue on the machine (no other measured job ran beside it), stages
scripts/m5pro/stageA.zsh, stageC.zsh, stageD.zsh, stageE.zsh in that order; every stage exited 0.
Libraries: cg31 = the fork's commit ff2538a (patches 0001-0039; CPU engine "multithreaded", GPU engine "metal-diamond"), stock =
openEMS v0.37.0, both as snapshots for bench/bench.py; the same library files as on the M5.
Method: bench/abtest.py, interleaved rounds, medians in MC/s; every run's port probes are compared with the reference run (c0).
Gate: wait up to 20 s for the other processes to use at most 300 % CPU (3 of 15 cores), redo a run once above that
(scripts/m5pro/common.zsh). Every row lists the other processes' median CPU / GPU use during its runs.
Background: macOS's own indexing (mediaanalysisd, Spotlight) ran all night; most rows ran beside 0.2 to 0.4 cores of other load.
Rows near or above one core: speed_notch_s1 (median 1.15 cores) and speed_patch_s5 (1.27), whose ranges are wider, and the
patch tutorial's row with the automatic count (speed_patch_s1 c5, 1.13). A job of another program (a PCB design rule check,
about 1.5 to 2 cores, no GPU) ran from about 07:29 to 07:36: it fell on round 3 of threads_patch_s5 (the medians of all four
rounds and of rounds 0 to 2 differ by 0.5 % at most: kept) and on round 0 of threads_kgcpw (its runs 0.7 to 14.5 % below the
median of rounds 1 to 3, though the others column of that round shows at most 0.9 cores; the GCPW row of the thread table uses
rounds 1 to 3, which moves no median by more than 1.8 %; both versions are printed).
Correctness: every CPU run of cg31 gave probes identical to its reference run; the GPU rows are within 1e-6 relative (1.7e-7 to
9.4e-7: Apple's GPU flushes subnormals, the CPU reference does not). The first night on this machine (2026-11-11, 21:21 to 21:35)
checked the same libraries: emskit fdtd check installed --lite and check cg32d --lite ALL IDENTICAL to stock (18 of 18 rows each,
six models), validate 15 of 15 on fastest and on metal-diamond, diamond-check 72 of 72, Debye check PASS. The tiled sweep's timing
on this machine is in results/m5pro_tiled_sweep_2026-11-12.txt.
